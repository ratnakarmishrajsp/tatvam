Last updated: July 29, 2026

Due to the immediate access nature of digital items (specifically ebooks, PDFs, checklists, and templates), all sales made on tatvam.shop are final. Once your transaction is finalized, the download link is instantly dispatched, rendering the item non-returnable.

2. Exceptional Circumstances

We want our readers to have a seamless experience. We will issue replacements or investigate refund claims in the following situations:

3. File Access Support

If you fail to receive your download email or encounter problems rendering the PDF on your specific mobile device, please forward your order details directly to our support desk. We will manually deliver alternative links or target formats within 24 hours.
